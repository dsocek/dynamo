Single-POST CMAF Binary Streaming

This client opens one POST /v1/videos/stream/binary/cmaf request and appends the returned init.mp4 + .m4s fragments into a browser MediaSource timeline.

Drop a storyboard .json here
or click to pick a file

Format: {"board_name": [{"prompt": ..., "latents": 18, "transition": "cut"}, ...]}. latents defaults to 25, transition to cut (or continue to carry the shot on), and an optional seed to 42. Several boards in one file: the first is used and the line above says which. Keep latents ≤ 21 — that is the attention window, and nothing server-side rejects a larger value. Resolution is fixed at 832x480.
